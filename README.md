# 📊 Sales Data Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to understand business performance, sales trends, and profitability. The analysis transforms raw sales records into meaningful insights that can support data-driven business decisions.

The project explores sales performance across different years, months, states, customers, product categories, and sub-categories through data analysis, pivot tables, and dashboard-oriented reporting.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Compare sales across states and product categories.
- Identify the most profitable customers.
- Evaluate product and sub-category performance.
- Support business decision-making through data visualization.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data analysis and reporting
- **Pivot Tables** — Summarizing and aggregating sales data
- **Charts and Data Visualization** — Presenting trends and comparisons
- **Data Cleaning** — Preparing data for analysis
- **Business Intelligence** — Converting data into actionable insights

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 9,993 sales records with 11 columns, including:

- Order Date
- Month and Year
- Customer Name
- State
- Category and Sub-Category
- Product Name
- Sales
- Quantity
- Profit

The records cover the period from January 2020 to December 2023.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Sales:** 2,296,919.70
- **Total Profit:** 286,409.85
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 37,871
- **Total Customers:** 793 unique customer names
- **Total Products:** 1,850 unique product names
- **Product Categories:** 3

*Note: Monetary values are presented in the dataset's original units; the currency is not specified.*

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Sales by State
Compare sales across states to identify high-performing geographical markets.

### 2. Top Profitable Customers
Identify customers contributing the most profit to the business.

### 3. Yearly Profit Analysis
Evaluate annual profitability and compare performance across product categories.

### 4. Monthly Sales Analysis
Explore monthly sales patterns and identify seasonal trends.

### 5. Category and Sub-Category Analysis
Compare sales across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology, as well as individual product sub-categories.

### 6. Sales Dashboard
Bring important performance indicators and analytical views together for easier business reporting.

## 💡 Business Value

This project demonstrates how spreadsheet-based analysis can help businesses monitor performance, identify profitable customers, compare product categories, understand regional sales patterns, and make informed decisions.
